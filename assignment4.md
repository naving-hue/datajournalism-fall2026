### TOP 10 ROLLER COASTERS REDESIGN

<img width="2965" height="2256" alt="TOP_10_ROLLER_COASTERS_REDESIGN" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7194c0a2-8cf8-4f96-8a91-94775c3b6546" />

For this redesign, I chose to go with an Ordered Column type of data visualization. I did this because the Ordered Column is a sorted version of the standard Column Chart making it useful for rankings, which this was. I chose the column over the bar (vertical over horizontal) because this was a measure of heights so it seemed intuitive to choose the vertical option at the cost of the awkwardly shifted wording. I included colors based on colors on the flag/associated with each country so blue for USA, green for Spain, red for Japan, and orange for Canada. I made the height meter go up to 420 feet intentionally, due to the specific values I was given. If I made it too large the heights would become indistinguishable and I couldn't go below the largest value (418 feet) so I settled on 420 feet as the max because it was the minimum value that could show the incremental changes between the data values that were closer together. Finally, I labeled my graphic as the same as the original, centering the title and making it more, personally, aesthetically pleasing. The original graphic had a few peaks that seemed a bit meaningless and the connection between the data points seemed like inappropriate use of the Bump data visualization.

### Chart 2 | *1700+ Missions: A Decade of Space Launches (2017-2026)* by Iris Sun
Original - [The World's Space Launch Sites](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/iris6683/viz/MoM2026Week26SpaceLaunch/MOM2026Week26) 

### SPACE LAUNCHES REDESIGN

<img width="3235" height="2491" alt="SPACE_LAUNCHES_REDESIGN" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5cf7a9c2-f8b5-4cbe-a9f6-60cbd4ce8e3d" />

For this redesign, I decided to go with a Dot Density type of data visualization. I selected this because the point of the graphic was to showcase the specific locations of data points and I found it relevant to put them on a map that showed them in reference to one another. The original graphic had its title left-aligned on the top of the page with a small subtitle. The graphic below it represented the 1700+ launches since 2017, but the category for this folder was *The World's Space Launch Sites*. Due to this, I chose to change the graphic type from lines that stemmed to a Dot Strip Plot type of visualization, to a map with a Dot Density style. My graphic had a world map along with dots for all launch sites around the world. I chose to use 4 different colors to distinguish between the countries even further and because the original graphic highlighted the US, China and Russia in particular. I chose red for China because the flag is mainly red, blue for USA because it is a color in the flag, grey for Russia because I didn't want to use white, and black for the "other" category representing the rest of the world. Finally, I decided to center the titled text and make the years into the subtitle for personal aesthetic purposes.
